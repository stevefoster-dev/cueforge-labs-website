The safe loop

Define outputs, build the wall, export the show pack

CuePatch keeps the processor truth and the physical truth in one document, so the map handed to the video engineer matches the cases that go on the truck.

  1. 01

    Define outputs

    Set processor output resolution and add only the outputs the design actually needs.

  2. 02

    Build screens

    Place real tiles from the panel database, size the grid, and align multiple surfaces on one canvas.

  3. 03

    Map and slice

    Assign screens to processor outputs and split surfaces across outputs with visual slice tools.

  4. 04

    Cable and pack

    Work out data runs and signal flow, then turn panel count into road-case and truck figures.

  5. 05

    Export

    Emit the images, mesh, media-server and lighting files the rest of the departments need.

Capabilities

What it does on a real show build

Get the wall right

Panel-accurate surfaces

Build from real product specs instead of nominal rectangles, so pixel pitch, panel count and physical size stay honest from the first sketch.

  • 48+ LED tile types covering ROE Visual, Absen, Unilumin, INFiLED, Gloshine and CHAUVET
  • Multiple screens positioned and aligned on a single canvas
  • Curved wall builder with configurable radius and panel count
  • Half-tile grid increments where the product supports them
  • Reference image overlays for design alignment

Answer the engineer

Processor outputs and slicing

Map surfaces onto processor outputs and see the split before anyone patches a fibre.

  • Multi-output mapping with visual screen slicing
  • Cable manager for data runs and signal flow
  • Built around Brompton, Novastar and Megapixel Helios processor workflows
  • Per-screen names, resolution readouts and master offsets

Answer the production manager

Weight, power and pack

The same document carries the numbers that decide rigging, power distribution and freight.

  • Live stats for resolution, pixel count, power draw and weight
  • Viewing-distance calculation that updates as the grid changes
  • Pack planner for road-case counts and truck layout
  • 3D preview with arena, stadium, festival, theatre, club and corporate presets

Hand it off

Export into the departments

Every downstream department gets a file of its own rather than a screenshot of yours.

  • Images at exact pixel resolution — PNG, JPG, WebP
  • OBJ mesh for 3D workflows
  • disguise CSV and 3D position data
  • grandMA3 XML and MVR for lighting
  • Slice summaries and pack-planner reports

Field fit

Who it is for — and where it stops

Built for

  • Video designers and screens producers scoping a wall during pre-production
  • Video engineers who need the processor map settled before load-in
  • Production managers pricing panel count, power, weight and trucking
  • Shops preparing a repeatable pack for a recurring design

Outside its authority

  • It does not drive processors, send content, or control anything live
  • It does not replace a site survey, rigging plot or structural engineering
  • It does not validate delivered media — that is CueCheck's job
  • It plans LED surfaces; whole-production logistics live in CuePack

Availability

Commercial truth, stated plainly

Private Validation — Beta Interest

CuePatch is not on public sale.

The application is in private validation with working professionals. There is no public download, no self-service activation, and no published price. Tier structures that previously appeared on this page are retired and are not CueForge's commercial position.

The intended shape is one complete professional edition licensed to one active computer, with a humane self-service transfer when a machine dies. Exact price and terms remain an open commercial decision and will not be quoted until they are settled.
