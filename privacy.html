Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 12, 2026

1. Who We Are

CueForge Labs LLC ("CueForge Labs," "we," "us") is a Connecticut-based software and production technology company. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website, Platform tools, and services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Name, email address, organization name, role
  • Payment information: Billing address and payment details (processed by our payment provider — we do not store full card numbers)
  • Platform data: Equipment inventories, configurations, project files, and other operational data you input into the Platform ("Your Content")
  • Communications: Emails, support requests, and feedback you send us

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Features used, frequency, session duration
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Log data: IP address, access times, pages viewed, referring URL

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use tracking cookies for advertising
  • We do not collect biometric data
  • We do not purchase data from third-party brokers

4. AI and Automated Processing

Transparency commitment: CueForge Labs uses AI and automated systems to power certain Platform features (e.g., predictive maintenance, pattern analysis, content validation). When Your Content is processed by these systems:

  • Processing occurs on our infrastructure or through contracted sub-processors bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Your Content is never used to train general-purpose AI models
  • Your Content is never shared with third parties for their own purposes
  • You can request information about which features use automated processing

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, payment processing, and email services that help us operate — all bound by data protection agreements
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or court order
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (you would be notified)
  • With your consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments
  • Employee access limited to need-to-know basis

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users within 60 days of discovery, as required by the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, or sooner where required by other applicable laws. Notification will include the nature of the breach, the data involved, and steps we are taking to address it.

7. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained for the duration of the service relationship
  • After termination: Your Content available for export for 30 days, then deleted
  • Backups: Purged from backup systems within 90 days of deletion
  • Legal obligations: Financial records retained as required by law (typically 7 years)

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data we hold
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export Your Content in standard formats
  • Object to specific processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at steve@cueforgelabs.com.

9. International Data

Our services are primarily operated from the United States. If you access the Platform from outside the US, your information may be transferred to and processed in the US. By using the Platform, you consent to this transfer.

10. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for use by individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Connecticut Data Privacy Act (CTDPA)

As a Connecticut-based company, we comply with the Connecticut Data Privacy Act. In addition to the rights listed in Section 8, Connecticut residents have the right to:

  • Opt out of profiling: You may opt out of any automated processing of personal data used for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Note: CueForge Labs does not currently engage in such profiling.
  • Opt out of sale: CueForge Labs does not sell personal data.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising: CueForge Labs does not use personal data for targeted advertising.
  • Appeal: If we deny your data rights request, you may appeal by contacting us at steve@cueforgelabs.com with "CTDPA Appeal" in the subject line. We will respond within 60 days.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated to active users at least 30 days before taking effect. The "Effective Date" at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
