The safe loop

Gear becomes cases, cases become a load that actually fits

CuePack models the three real layers of a production — what you own, what it travels in, and what carries it — so a pack plan can be checked before the truck is booked rather than after it is full.

  1. 01

    Inventory

    Record the gear a production is actually taking, item by item.

  2. 02

    Case up

    Assign gear into road cases, racks and carts with real dimensions and weight limits.

  3. 03

    Plan the pack

    Place cases into trucks, ULDs or containers and watch weight and volume as you go.

  4. 04

    Check and secure

    Review the packed load in 3D, resolve axle-weight and securing questions.

  5. 05

    Publish and reuse

    Issue the manifest, then keep the plan as the starting point for the next leg.

Capabilities

Built around how productions actually move

Know what you have

Gear, cases and continuity

The three-tier model — gear, cases, pack — keeps the warehouse truth and the tour truth in the same place instead of in one person's head.

  • Gear inventory tracked at item level, including serial numbers
  • Case library with real dimensions, weight limits and utilisation
  • Built-in road-case reference data for common case makes
  • Project templates so a recurring design does not start from zero

Make it fit

Pack planning in every mode

One planner covers the transport modes a real production mixes on the same leg.

  • Drag-and-drop case placement into trucks and trailers
  • Air freight against ULD containers and pallet types
  • Sea containers with weight-versus-volume trade-offs
  • Combined multi-mode movements for international routing
  • Real-time 3D preview of the packed container

Stay legal and safe

Weight, axles and securing

The numbers that get a load stopped at a scale are calculated while the plan is still editable.

  • Axle weight distribution for road compliance
  • Load bar and strap requirement calculations
  • Weight and volume totals per container and per movement

Run the department

Documents and shared access

A production is a group activity, so the plan has to survive being handed around.

  • Manifests and pack documentation generated from the live plan
  • Multi-user collaboration across a production account
  • Venue and movement logistics captured alongside the pack
  • Operational history retained between legs and between shows

Field fit

Who it is for — and where it stops

Built for

  • Production managers planning trucking, freight and load-out order
  • Rental houses turning shop inventory into a repeatable tour pack
  • Crew chiefs who need one authoritative case list per department
  • Touring operations that repeat a design across legs and territories

Outside its authority

  • It is not a freight broker, carrier booking system or customs filing service
  • It does not replace a rigging plot, weight certificate or legal load inspection
  • It is not an accounting system; settlement stays with the production's finance tools
  • LED surface design belongs in CuePatch, not here

How CuePack will be sold

Productions are seasonal, so CuePack is not sold as a flat per-seat subscription. The approved commercial architecture has four parts, and the exact amounts for each are still being set. No price is quoted until they are.

  • Account membership. The workspace, the gear and case library, and the operational history stay alive whether or not a production is currently out.
  • Active production packs. You pay for the productions that are actually running, not for a permanent seat count.
  • Scale add-ons. Larger truck-scale movements are priced above the standard production pack.
  • Standby and archive. A reduced state that keeps a dark production's history and templates available for the next leg.

Onboarding, implementation and support are quoted separately from the software. There is no public checkout, and CuePack is not billed automatically today.
