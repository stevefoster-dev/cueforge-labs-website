Every Transport Mode

🚛

Truck Packs

Sprinter vans, box trucks, semi trailers. Drag-and-drop cases, auto-pack algorithm, weight distribution warnings, axle weight calculations.

✈️

Air Freight

Cargo aircraft (747F, 777F), ULD containers (LD3, LD7, PMC pallets). Calculate planeloads automatically.

🚢

Sea Freight

20'/40'/40'HC shipping containers. Weight vs volume optimization for international tours.

🚌

Tour Buses

Bunk assignments, bus stock management, driver HOS tracking, team driving support, separate routing from trucks.

📦

Case Database

58KB of road case specs built in. LED carts, racks, custom cases. Track weight utilization per case.

🧊

3D Visualization

Real-time 3D view of packed containers. Orbit, zoom, pan. Color-coded by department. Weight heat maps.
