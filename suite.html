Questions

What platforms are supported?
macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel). Windows versions are on the roadmap.
Do I need an audio interface?
CueRoll and CueMaker need an audio interface with LTC input. Any interface that shows up in macOS audio settings works. CuePatch doesn't need one.
Is there a free trial?
CuePatch has a free version with single-surface support. For CueRoll and CueMaker, email us for a demo — we'll walk you through it live.
What about code signing?
Apps aren't notarized yet (indie dev life). On first launch: right-click → Open → Open. After that it runs normally. We're working on notarization.
Can I upgrade from individual to suite?
Yes — email us with your original purchase info and we'll credit what you've already paid toward the suite price.
Do you offer team/site licenses?
Absolutely. Email us for volume pricing — we'll work something out that makes sense for your shop.
