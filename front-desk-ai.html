02

What it captures

A missed call is not a message. It is an unfinished decision.

Voicemail hands you a recording and a callback you will forget. Front Desk AI hands you a record with the request, the location, the timing, the urgency, the questions nobody answered, and a recommendation you can approve or throw away. Every screen on this page is an exact capture of the same rehearsal workspace running fictional data.

Job — know what to do first

The owner's desk, in priority order

Today opens on the count that matters — what was captured, what needs review, what has escalated — and then lists the actions themselves with a due time and a Review control on each one. The qualified opportunity is deliberately first.

  • Captured, needs-review and escalation counts across the top of the day
  • Next actions carrying an explicit priority and a due time, each opening for review
  • An appointment request held as “requested, pending owner review” rather than booked
  • A quote follow-up shown as a draft that has not been sent, accepted or paid

Boundary The recommendation is a recommendation. Front Desk creates or updates a structured opportunity; it never issues a binding quote, never confirms an appointment the customer merely asked for, and never commits you to work.

Job — keep the customer

The record outlives the phone call

The call does not stop at a transcript. The customer becomes a durable record in Client Hub with their address, their tags, their conversations, their work and their history — so the second call already knows about the first one, whoever picks it up.

  • One dossier per customer: people, conversations, work, documents and money
  • Current work shown with its stage and an explicit “pricing not set” state
  • Intake documents attached to the customer and marked as needing review
  • An activity trail recording what the software did and what it left for a person

Boundary Client Hub is the shared record layer for the whole toolkit, not a Front Desk feature. Switching Front Desk off later does not delete the customers or the history it wrote.

03

The safe loop

Capture, structure, surface — then wait for you

The loop is deliberately short of the commitment. Everything up to the decision is automated; the decision itself is not.

  1. 01

    Capture

    An inbound call that would otherwise have become voicemail is answered and captured as a conversation with its channel and its timestamp.

  2. 02

    Structure

    The conversation becomes an intent, a disposition, a summary and the specific details a service business needs — what is wrong, where it is, and how soon it matters.

  3. 03

    Qualify honestly

    A sales pitch is logged as a vendor solicitation rather than becoming a fake customer. What the caller did not answer is written down as an open question instead of being invented.

  4. 04

    Connect

    The customer, the opportunity and the job are created or updated in Client Hub, so the record is one thread rather than a copy in another tool.

  5. 05

    Surface for decision

    A recommended next action appears on the owner's desk with a priority and a due time. If a notification fails, the failure becomes its own visible task.

Built for

  • Owner-operators whose phone rings while both hands are already busy
  • Service businesses losing work to voicemail rather than to competitors
  • Small teams who need one shared answer to “who was that, and what did they want?”
  • Anyone whose follow-ups currently live in a notebook, a text thread and a memory

What it never does

  • It does not issue a binding quote or quote a price on your behalf
  • It does not confirm an appointment the customer only requested
  • It does not commit you to work, to a date or to a cost
  • It does not send anything commercial without an owner decision
  • It is optional — the rest of the toolkit does not require it
